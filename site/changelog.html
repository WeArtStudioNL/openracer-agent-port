Changelog

What changed in every version of the game. A Hotfix only fixes things: its last number goes up (2.3.0 → 2.3.1). The version you play is shown at the bottom of the main menu. Every detail is in CHANGELOG.md on GitHub.

2.4.0 Release 1 November 2026

  • Smarter AI: it stops behind a slower car instead of running into it, waits for a car that spun and then goes round it, brakes in time for the bends, and drives with the grip its tyres have left. Normal is no longer as fast as Hard after a few laps, and the opponents are of your kind: cars, karts or motorcycles.
  • Your start on the grid: pole, random or last, in the race options. Restart is in the pause menu.
  • First race: a short panel with the keys that matter. A race pauses when the window loses the focus and while the tuning menu is open.
  • Gamepad: the menus, the results, the tuning menu in a race and the Load window of the garage work with a controller; X switches the camera, Back repairs, and the buttons have their names.
  • Damage: dents are as deep as the hit (they were twice as deep), and a crumple zone takes the rest.
  • Engine building: an engine swap changes the clutch, the sound, the weight and the fuel use at once; a turbo engine is no longer counted twice.
  • Replays and video: an edit keeps the damage, the smoke, the skid marks and the camera of every clip; Save as mp4 can be stopped; a replay keeps the setup of every car.
  • Track editor: Test drive and Export zip; saving says what a track still needs; the AI no longer drives an old lap after the road was changed.
  • Car editor: New makes a new car, Export zip for every car, the test bench works for a new car, and the shell, the driver and the parts keep what you set.
  • Tuning menu: setups bring all their gears, Default compares with the car as made, and the menu fits a phone.
  • Garage: zips from Finder, Windows and with a .gltf import; several zips at once; a linked folder says what it found and when it needs permission again.
  • Skidpad and drag strip: the skidpad measures the same on every screen; the drag strip Top 50 shows R/T and the time of every distance.
  • Fixed: a frame without time turned a car into numbers that are not numbers for the rest of the race; a car with an endless angle could freeze the game.

2.3.0 Release 1 November 2026

  • Fixed: the screen went black after a crash into an AI car, or when you drove through your own ghost. A shell of a car that blew up, or one bad pixel in the reflections, made the lighting black for the rest of the race.
  • Tyres with tread: a custom tyre has a tread slider from road over all-terrain to knobbly. More tread grips more on grass, sand, gravel and mud, and less on tarmac.
  • Tyre detail (Esc → Simulation): Medium and Low make the physics of the cars' tyres up to 2.5 times cheaper, for slower computers and phones. Full stays the default.
  • Hide the driver in the cockpit camera: a checkbox under Tuning → Camera, kept with the tune.
  • Track editor: walls, tyre stacks, cones, trees and the other prefabs can be placed on a track with its own model.
  • F9 checks what makes the picture dark, for when something goes wrong with the graphics.
  • three.js r187, the graphics library under the game.

2.2.0 Release 31 October 2026

  • Soft body and damage: every car is a shell of nodes and beams that dents, parts come loose on hinges or fall off, wheels bend or come off, and cars dent each other. Damage can be switched off; Backspace repairs.
  • Motorcycles with a rider who leans and moves on the bike, and a ragdoll crash.
  • Weather and time of day: clear, cloudy, fog, rain and snow, from dawn to night, with a wet or snowy road that grips less.
  • A new main menu in steps: the way to play, the car and its setup, the track, the weather, the race and the opponents.
  • Drag strip with a tree of lights, a time slip and a Top 50.
  • Replays in a library, takes, and a video editor.
  • Engine builder and dyno in the car editor and the tuning menu, and setups saved under a name.
  • New gearboxes: IMT, AMT, dual clutch, torque converter and CVT.
  • The tyre is a shell of nodes, and the 2D physics is gone.
  • New cars and tracks: the Hatchback, two karts, the OpenRacer Muscle, the stadium truck, two motorcycles; the Desert, the Desert drag strip, Kartbaan Oldenzaal and Short track.

2.1.0 Release 19 October 2026

  • Replay: everything is recorded from the start; Esc → Replay plays it back with a slider, rewind, pause and speeds from 0.1× to 5×.
  • Cameras in the replay: fixed, follow, cockpit and a free camera.
  • Save and share a replay as a file; nothing goes to a server.
  • Fixed: the end and the sides of a ramp were walls; the sky trembled; an error while a game was loading.

2.0.1 Hotfix 19 October 2026

  • Touch controls for phones and tablets, and a phone no longer runs out of memory.
  • A new version reaches the player at once, and the helper server, the names in the menus and the site were made safer.

2.0.0 Release 19 October 2026

  • The fork as a new version: the driving model, races with AI, ghost and records, wheels with force feedback, the car and track editors, the tuning menu, the skidpad and the local garage.
