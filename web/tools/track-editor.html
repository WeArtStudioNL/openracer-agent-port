Reference imagenone

A picture to draw the track over: a map or an aerial photo (png, jpg or webp). It lies flat in the view, is only for this editor and is saved with the track. Scale by two points: click two places on the picture whose distance you know (the length of a straight, a building) and type that distance.

A .glb, or a .gltf with its .bin and textures (select them all). Make the track in metres, y up. Importing a model in a track that exists replaces its model.

Surfaces

Road, pit lane, grass and ground can be driven on, scenery is driven into (walls, buildings), decor is only to look at (sky, trees). Click a part in the view to find it.

Start and lap gates

Use the tools in the view: click on the road and drag in the direction that the car drives. Grid box places the next box of the start grid: box 1 is the start of the player, 2 to 8 are for the opponents of a race. The first gate is the finish line.

Pit boxes

A box of 4 by 8 metres for every player, yellow and see-through in the game, so that everybody knows which box is theirs. Choose Pit box in the toolbar, click on the road (or beside it) where the box is and drag in the direction that the car faces. Box 1 is the box of the player, 2 to 8 are those of the opponents, in the order of the grid. A car that stands still with all four wheels inside its box is serviced (fuel and tyres); the AI drives to its box and back. Without a box of its own (fewer boxes than cars) a car can use any box.

Pit line: the path that the AI cars follow to their box and back, so that they know where to slow down and do not follow the racing line into the wall. Choose Pit line in the toolbar and click on the road, in the order that a car drives: the first point on the racing line where the pit lane starts, then through the pit lane past the boxes (the line should pass through the middle of every box: the car stops at the point of the line that is nearest to its box), and the last point back on the racing line. The line is smoothed; a point every 5 to 15 metres is enough.

Objects

The library of your own models (glb: a barrel, a block, a tree), the same in every track (the folder objects/: it needs the server, start-server.sh). Click a model and you are placing it: press on the road and drag to say which way it faces; Escape goes back to Look. Add a new one with +; it is kept in the library with its picture. Choose a placed object in the Layers panel (or click it in the view): W moves, E turns, R scales, Delete takes it away. The origin of a model is put at the middle of its base (or keep the origin of the file); a rigid object sleeps until a car hits it, then it is pushed and falls; a model that is not rigid stays where it is. Its settings are below the library.

The prefabs of the track designer (walls, tyre stacks, cones, trees, a house, a grandstand): click one to place it. A wall or a cone stands in the track like a model; a tree or a bush only shows.

Curbsnone

Curbstones along the edge of the road, red and white (or another colour). Draw curb: click on the road for every node, along the edge; the curb is a smooth line through them, and Enter or Escape ends it. Every node lies on the surface under it. Afterwards: click a node and move it with the arrows of the gizmo, Shift + click on the road adds a node to the chosen curb (between the two nearest nodes, or at an end), Delete takes the chosen node away and joins the nodes beside it. A curb without nodes is gone. In the game a curb is road, a few centimetres high.

Cinematic camerasnone

Cameras for the replay (the camera button Cinematic): use Add camera in the view and click on the track. Click a camera to choose it and move it with the gizmo. The button above a camera changes its range, a circle on the floor: move it with the node in the middle, set the radius with the node on the edge. In the replay the camera whose circle the car is in is used (else the nearest), and it always turns to follow the car. The fields of a camera are x, y, z and the radius of the range.

AI racing line

The line that the AI cars drive. Record it by driving: the game opens with this track, you drive a calm lap through all the gates (every lap you finish replaces the line), and Esc or Stop saves the last lap as the line and brings you back here.

A recorded line is drawn in the view (yellow, with arrows in the direction it is driven). Edit the line shows its nodes (about 8 m apart): click a node and move it with the arrows of the gizmo, Shift + click on the road adds a node, Delete takes the chosen one away, Apply line saves it, Cancel keeps it as it was. Draw the line makes a new line without driving: click on the road, one node after the other in the direction that the AI drives (the line closes itself from the last node to the first, so go all the way round), at least four; Undo the last node takes the last one back, Shift + click adds a node between two others, and Apply line saves it in place of the line that is there (a smooth curve through the nodes, a point every 2 m). Put the nodes in the middle of the road, closer together in the bends.

Sky and light

A track of the old format has its own sky in its model: leave this off to keep it. Switch off the colours of the surfaces to see the model in this light.

In the game the sky, the fog, the sun and the time of day come from the player's weather and time settings, the same on every track. Of this card the game uses only the strength of the sun and of the ambient light (how bright the materials of this track want the light). The rest is the light in this editor, and for an older version of the game.

Click on the ground to add a node between the two nodes it is nearest to; drag a node to move it, and its arrow up or down for hills. The road is smooth through the nodes. The textures tile in metres and go into the glb with the track. Ctrl+Z undoes.

Pick a prefab: a click on the ground puts one down, a dragged line makes a row of them (walls, tyres, trees…) that follows the line. Drag a prefab to move it; it sits on the ground, the road or what it is put on. The ring turns a single one; the points of a row can be dragged.
