A .glb, or a .gltf with its .bin and textures (select them all). Make the track in metres, y up. Importing a model in a track that exists replaces its model.

Surfaces

Road, pit lane, grass and ground can be driven on, scenery is driven into (walls, buildings), decor is only to look at (sky, trees). Click a part in the view to find it.

Start and lap gates

Use the tools in the view: click on the road and drag in the direction that the car drives. The first gate is the finish line.

AI racing line

The line that the AI cars drive. Record it by driving: the game opens with this track, you drive a calm lap through all the gates (every lap you finish replaces the line), and Esc or Stop saves the last lap as the line and brings you back here.

Sky and light

A track of the old format has its own sky in its model: leave this off to keep it. Switch off the colours of the surfaces to see the model in this light.

Click on the ground to add a node between the two nodes it is nearest to; drag a node to move it, and its arrow up or down for hills. The road is smooth through the nodes. The textures tile in metres and go into the glb with the track. Ctrl+Z undoes.

Pick a prefab: a click on the ground puts one down, a dragged line makes a row of them (walls, tyres, trees…) that follows the line. Drag a prefab to move it; it sits on the ground, the road or what it is put on. The ring turns a single one; the points of a row can be dragged.
