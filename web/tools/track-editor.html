Track editor

Make a track for OpenRacer from a glTF or glb model (for example from Blender): say what every part is made of, put the start and the lap gates on the road and save it. It shows up in the main menu of the game. Back to the game

Track

Choose a track to change its start, gates and name (a track of the old format can be changed this way too), or import a model below. Saving a track that was chosen here overwrites it.

Model

Pick a .glb file, or a .gltf together with its .bin and texture files (select them all at once). Make the track in metres, y is up (Blender’s glTF export does this). Importing a model in a track that exists replaces its model.

Surfaces

Say what every part is made of. Road, pit lane, grass and ground can be driven on (each with its own grip), scenery is something the car drives into (walls, buildings), decor is only to look at (sky, trees, flags). Click a part in the 3D view to find it in the list.

Sky and light

The sky, the fog and the sun of the track. Switch off “Show the surfaces in colour” above to see the real colours of the model in this light. A track of the old format has its own sky in its model: leave this off to keep it.

Start and lap gates

Choose a tool, then click on the road and drag in the direction that the car drives. The car starts at the start; a lap is counted when it drives through all the gates in order. The first gate is the finish line: put it just in front of the start.
