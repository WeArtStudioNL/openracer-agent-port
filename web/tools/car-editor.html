Select the parts of each wheel (tyre, rim, disc…); Ctrl or Cmd selects more.

Rims and tyres from the wheel library

A rim or a tyre of web/wheels/wheels.json takes the place of the wheel parts of its axle (their middle is the hub). A tyre of the library brings its own shell (the rings and nodes of the tread, the inner shell, the carcass): the shell sliders of the Tuning card then do nothing for that axle (the line below gives the shell of the tyre).

The front end of a motorcycle is found by name: steerhead_… turns with the steering, brake_…_FL goes with the front wheel along the fork, swingarm turns with the rear wheel, and the rider takes grip_L/R, footpeg_L/R, frontbrake_R, clutch_L and shifter_L (see MOTORCYCLE.md).
Suspension and driveline

The suspension built from loose parts (web/parts/<type>/<part>.glb, see the README): pick a type for each axle, where the engine is, and drag the mounts in the view. The drive (front, rear, all) is in the Tuning card. "From the model" keeps the parts of your own model (named susp_…).

A propshaft runs from the gearbox to every driven axle that is more than 40 cm from it.

A mount: W moves, E turns (as the exhaust points). The parts of a stretched kind (arms, springs, shafts) are aimed from their mount at their other end and stretched to it; a turn of such a mount rolls the part about its length.

Soft body: material and strengths

What the shell is made of. An empty field is the default (shown grey). A hit pushes a node: it springs back up to the yield force, bends for good above it (the energy is gone) and tears off at the break force. Parts that swing or come loose

Keep this panel open: select nodes in the view (or click on the model) and use the Set buttons next to the fields.

What the fields mean

A part is an object of the model (a door, the hood, a mirror, a wing). Hinge: it turns about an axis, between two angles, with a spring that brings it back to its rest angle and a damper. With a latch force it sits shut until the load on it passes that force, with a break force it tears off then. Loose: it sits on the car until the load passes the break force, and then it is a free object that falls and slides. The load is the mass times the acceleration of its place, or the force of the shell on the nodes it hangs on, whichever is more. The origin and the axis are in the frame of the car: x to the left, y up, z forward, from the centre of gravity; with the shell shown, the arrows in the view are the hinges.

Materials

The materials of the model are physically based and used as they are. A model does not always arrive the way it looked in Blender (the glass of many models becomes opaque white metal): set such a material right with a preset or by hand. The changes are saved with the car.

Engine sound settings

The gas in every cylinder is simulated: compression, the burn, the valves, the exhaust runners and the collector. The simulation rate sets how much of the computer it costs; lower it if the sound stutters.
