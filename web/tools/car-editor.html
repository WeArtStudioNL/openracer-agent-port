Parts that swing or come loose

Select nodes in the view, then Set
What the fields mean

A part is an object of the model (a door, the hood, a mirror, a wing). Hinge: it turns about an axis, between two angles, with a spring that brings it back to its rest angle and a damper. With a latch force it sits shut until the load on it passes that force, with a break force it tears off then. Loose: it sits on the car until the load passes the break force, and then it is a free object that falls and slides. The load is the mass times the acceleration of its place, or the force of the shell on the nodes it hangs on, whichever is more. The origin and the axis are in the frame of the car: x to the left, y up, z forward, from the centre of gravity; with the shell shown, the arrows in the view are the hinges.
