Materials

The materials of the model are physically based and used as they are. A model does not always arrive the way it looked in Blender (the glass of many models becomes opaque white metal): set such a material right with a preset or by hand. The changes are saved with the car.

Engine sound settings

The gas in every cylinder is simulated: compression, the burn, the valves, the exhaust runners and the collector. The simulation rate sets how much of the computer it costs; lower it if the sound stutters.
