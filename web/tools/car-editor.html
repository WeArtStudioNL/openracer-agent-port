Car editor

Make a car for OpenRacer from a glTF or glb model (for example from Blender), set it up and save it. It shows up in the main menu of the game. Back to the game

Car

Choose a car to change it, or make a new one below. Saving a car that was chosen here overwrites it.

Model

Pick a .glb file, or a .gltf together with its .bin and texture files (select them all at once). The model has to face forward along the blue arrow, up is y. Blender exports a car that looks towards -Y (the front view) correctly; otherwise turn it here.
No model loaded.
The origin of the model, after turning, scaling and the offset, has to be at the height of the wheel axles, in the middle between the axles. “Fit” does that.

Steering wheel

A part of the model called steeringwheel turns with the steering, around its own middle. The axis is found from the shape of the part (the direction in which it is flat).
No model loaded.

Wheels

The wheels steer and spin, so they have to be separate parts of the model. Select, for each wheel, the part or parts (tyre, rim, brake disc…) that belong to it. Hold Ctrl or Cmd to select more.

Materials

The materials of the model are physically based and are used as they are. A model does not always arrive the way it looked in Blender (the glass of many models becomes opaque white metal): set such a material right here, with a preset or by hand. The changes are saved with the car.

Cameras

The cameras of the game, per car: 1 is the fixed camera behind the car, 2 follows the direction of the car smoothly and widens its field of view with the speed, 3 is in the car. The dots in the view show where the cameras are (heights are above the road, the cockpit is from the origin of the model: x to the left, y up, z forward). Fields of view are the vertical angle in degrees.

Engine sound

The sound is calculated from a model of the engine, in one of two ways. Pipes: every cylinder has pipes for the intake and the exhaust in which the pressure wave of the burn travels up and down and is partly reflected, and the exhausts come together in a pipe, a muffler and the outlet (after the engine sound generator of Antonio-R1). Combustion: the gas in every cylinder is simulated (compression, the burn, the valves, the exhaust runners and the collector) and the pressure in the exhaust is played through the recorded impulse response of an exhaust (after engine-sim of Ange Yaghi; it costs more of the computer). Press Play and change the values while you listen.

Instead of the calculated sound the car can play recordings of its engine, the way most games do it: loops of the engine at a steady speed that are played at a pitch that follows the engine speed and fade into each other. Take a few seamless loops (for example at idle, at 2500, at 4500 and at the rev limit) and say at which engine speed each was recorded, and whether the throttle was open (on) or closed (off). One kind (on) is enough. Without recordings the sound below is used.
