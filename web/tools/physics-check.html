Physics check

Runs the physics of the game for the cars you pick, on a track, and says what is wrong: bounce after a landing, a wheel that flickers, a car that spins by itself, a bad ride height, a car that blows up. Run it after a change to the physics or to a car. It takes a few seconds for a car.

Limits: still bouncing 1.5 s after a 3 m landing (more than 0.5 m/s up) or a 10 m landing (1.5 m/s); wheels that flicker (let go of the road) at most 4 times in 10 s on the straight; the yaw rate in a bend that is held at most 1.15 of what the steering asks; a ride height within 8 cm of the one that the car is made for; no number that is not a number, and no speed that jumps.
