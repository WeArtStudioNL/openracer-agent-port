# Sounds

`tyre-squeal.ogg` (or `.mp3` / `.wav`): an optional recording of a tyre squeal that the game uses instead of the sound it makes itself.
Use a clean loop of 2 to 6 seconds of one tyre squealing at a steady level, without an engine or other sounds in it, and one that
loops without a click. The game changes its pitch with the speed of the car and its volume with how hard the tyres slide.
Take care that you may use the recording (for example a CC0 one).
